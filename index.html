LAMINAR30

The Laminar30 is not a conventional muffler. It was developed through repeated exhaust configurations and real-world testing to change the character of the exhaust without simply choking sound away.

Testing has included wide-open-throttle acceleration, cruise behavior, throttle-lift/deceleration characteristics and spectrograph analysis.

The result in testing has been a strong, clean exhaust note with reduced rasp and exhaust ringing, while remaining surprisingly quiet during cruise and off-throttle operation.

Construction: 16-gauge stainless steel
Welding: TIG welded • back-purged
Inlet: 3 inch
Outlet: 3 inch
Total length: Approximately 30 inches each
Testing: WOT, cruise, decel + frequency analysis
Development: Dayberry Blueprinting
